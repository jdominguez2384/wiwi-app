# WIWI 1.0 Review Notes

## Reviewer Summary

WIWI helps delivery, rideshare, and other gig workers estimate real shift earnings after mileage, fuel, work expenses, vehicle costs, and a user-selected tax reserve. The estimates are informational and are not tax, legal, or financial advice.

## Review Access

- A review account with representative shift data will be supplied in the private review credentials fields.
- The first-run tutorial can be replayed from Settings > Help & tutorial.
- Account deletion is available from Settings and at `https://getwiwi.com/delete-account`.
- Password recovery and account confirmation return to the app through the `wiwi://` URL scheme.

## In-App Purchases

WIWI Free includes unlimited core shift logging, history, and basic insights. WIWI Pro offers advanced comparisons, goal forecasts, reports, custom cost profiles, and shift notes/tags. Purchases and restores use the platform store through RevenueCat; no external purchase link is shown in the app.

## Permissions and Hardware

WIWI does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. Network access is required for account synchronization. Reports are generated from the user's own shift data and can be shared using the system share sheet.
